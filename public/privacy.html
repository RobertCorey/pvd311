PVD 311

Privacy

Community project — not affiliated with the City of Providence

PVD 311 is a free, volunteer-run project. We keep this simple: we collect what we need to file your report with the city, and nothing we don't. We never sell your data. Here's the whole picture.

The big thing to know: once we file your report with the city, it becomes a public record in the City of Providence's 311 system. Reports appear on the city's public request feed, where anyone can see them. We can't undo that.

What we collect, and why

  • Your report. The issue category, the address, the location coordinates (from your photo, your phone's GPS, or what you type), an optional description, an optional photo, and any per-category answers (for example, the size of a pothole). We collect this to file the report with the city — it's the report.
  • Your name and email — only if you give them. Both are optional; you can report anonymously. If you give your email, we pass it to the city so the city can send you its own updates on the report. Otherwise we only use it to reach you about that report. No newsletters, no marketing.
  • An anonymous device ID. Your device gets an anonymous ID (through Firebase). There's no login, no account, no name attached. We use it to space out submissions — one report every few minutes per device — and to show you your own reports under “My reports.”
  • Basic usage analytics. We log anonymous events (which steps get used, whether a submit succeeds) to see what's working and fix what isn't. No ads.
  • An offline copy on your device. If you submit while offline, the report is saved in your browser's storage on your own phone and sent automatically once you're back online. That copy never leaves your device until it's sent.

Where it lives

Your report and any photo are stored on Firebase / Google Cloud, in the United States. Google runs this infrastructure for us (a processor) — they store and move the data on our behalf; they don't get it to use for their own purposes. Photos go to Firebase Cloud Storage in a folder tied to your anonymous device ID.

Who sees it

  • The City of Providence. Once we file your report, it's a public record in the city's 311 system and on its public feed. This is the point of the app — but it means your report (photo, location, description, and any name you attached) is public once submitted.
  • Google, as the infrastructure provider described above.
  • The volunteer(s) running the project, to review reports and file them with the city.
  • Not sold. Ever. We don't sell your data and we don't share it with anyone beyond what's needed to file and track your report.

How long we keep it

  • Your report stays in our database so we can file it, track its status, and show it to you.
  • We keep proof screenshots of the submission for about 90 days, so we can confirm a report actually went through if there's a question about it.
  • Once a report is filed, the city keeps its own copy under the city's own rules. We don't control that copy.

Your choices

  • Stay anonymous. Name and email are optional. Leave them blank and we still file your report.
  • Ask us to delete our copy. Email pvdsnow@proton.me and we'll delete the copy on our side. We cannot delete what the city already holds — once a report is a public record with Providence, only the city can remove it.

Children

PVD 311 is meant for residents reporting city issues. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and change the date below.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a deletion request? Email pvdsnow@proton.me.
