PVD 311

About

Community project — not affiliated with the City of Providence

What this is

PVD 311 is a free tool that lets you report a problem in Providence — a pothole, a broken street light, a missed trash pickup, illegal dumping, an abandoned vehicle, a parking issue, and more — from your phone in about 30 seconds. You pick the issue, add a photo and a location, and we file it with the city's official 311 system for you.

Who runs it

We're a volunteer running this as a community project. We are not the City of Providence, and we're not affiliated with or endorsed by the city. We just wanted to make 311 reporting easier for our neighbors. The city's real 311 system is at 311.providenceri.gov.

How a report flows

  1. You submit. Choose the issue, snap a photo, confirm the address, and send it.
  2. We review it. Every report goes through a review — a mix of automated checks (is it inside Providence, is it a duplicate, does it have what the city needs) and a human when needed.
  3. We file it with 311. We submit it to the city's official 311 portal on your behalf, using a project-owned account.
  4. You get a case number. The city assigns a 311 case number and works your report like any other request. You can see your reports any time under My reports. If you gave your email, the city can also send you its own status updates.

Not for emergencies. If someone is in danger, call 911.

This isn't for anything urgent or hazardous — a gas leak, a downed power line, flooding, a fire. Call the city or the utility directly for those.

What we don't do

Reports are reviewed and filed by people and take some time — this is not instant, and it's not a substitute for calling the city for anything time-sensitive. For urgent issues, call 311 or use the official portal directly.

Seasonal issues

Some categories only show up when they're relevant. Winter issues like unshoveled sidewalks and unplowed streets appear roughly November through March and are hidden the rest of the year, so the list stays short and matches what the city is actually handling that season.

Prefer to file it yourself?

You can always report straight to the city — no middle step — at 311.providenceri.gov, or by phone. Using PVD 311 is entirely optional; it just saves you a few steps.
