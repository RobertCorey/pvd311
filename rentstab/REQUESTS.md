# Records requests: ready to send

Drafts for the by-request items in DATA_SOURCES.md §3, in priority order. Nothing here has been sent. Each asks for property-level records in an electronic format and for no personal information about tenants. Before sending, search the portal again (DATA_SOURCES.md, "Before filing"): someone may have asked since 2026-10-31.

APRA (R.I.G.L. 38-2) applies to the city, RIHousing and state agencies: 10 business days to respond (extendable by 20), first hour of search free, then up to $15/hour. Court records are not APRA records; see request 5.

---

## 1. Certificates of occupancy (Inspections & Standards)

**Send:** https://providenceri.nextrequest.com/ (department: Inspections & Standards)
**Why:** settles the new-construction exemption (10 years from the CO) exactly; today it's estimated from permits. Request 26-2355 (permits) came back the same day.

> Under the Access to Public Records Act, I request an electronic export (CSV or Excel) of all Certificate of Occupancy and Certificate of Use and Occupancy records in the City's permitting system (OpenGov / Viewpoint Cloud), all statuses, issued from January 1, 2012 to the present. For each record please include: record number, record type, status, issue date, property address, plat/lot (or parcel ID), number of dwelling units, use or occupancy type, and the associated building permit number. This is the same system and format as the permit export released for request 26-2355. I am not requesting applicant contact information. Please contact me if any part would incur a fee over $30.

## 2. Assessor package (Tax Assessor)

**Send:** https://providenceri.nextrequest.com/ (department: Tax Assessor)
**Why:** (a) is the biggest uncertainty in the coverage estimate: about 5,600 small buildings whose owner seems to live there but isn't on the owner-occupied rate, and an unusual loss of the rate across FY2020→FY2024 (DATA_REPORT.md §2). (b) is a list of 3+-unit rentals the city already holds.

> Under the Access to Public Records Act, I request the following records in electronic form (CSV or Excel):
>
> 1. Statistical data on owner-occupied (homestead) status changes for tax years 2020 through 2026: for each year, the number of residential parcels granted, renewed, revoked or not renewed for the owner-occupied rate or homestead exemption, and any recertification or re-application notice the Assessor sent around the FY2021 change from the owner-occupied levy code to the homestead exemption. (Request 25-1068 asked for similar data; no files were posted publicly.)
> 2. A list, by plat/lot and property address, of residential properties for which a non-owner-occupied landlord filed the fire insurance declaration required by state law for buildings of three or more households, with the number of units declared and the filing year. Owner names are not needed.
> 3. A list of all tax stabilization agreements (TSAs) currently in effect: plat/lot, address, ordinance or agreement number, start and end tax years, and number of residential units if recorded.
> 4. The Assessor's sales file for calendar years 2019 through 2023 used in the 2021 and 2024 revaluations, valid and invalid sales, with plat/lot, sale date, sale price, validity code, land use and number of units. Buyer and seller names are not needed.
> 5. The number of dwelling units and bedrooms per unit for each taxable residential property (as asked in request 24-1460, which shows no public files).
>
> Please release each part as it is ready rather than waiting for all five. Please contact me if any part would incur a fee over $30.

## 3. Case-level eviction records (R.I. Judiciary, not APRA)

**Send:** Form **Supreme-19**, "Request for Electronic Information or Report (Non-APRA)", to Community Outreach & Public Relations, Licht Judicial Complex, 250 Benefit Street, Providence, RI 02903, (401) 222-4580. Form: https://www.courts.ri.gov (Forms → Supreme Court → Supreme-19).
**Why:** eviction filings by building. Today we have filings by tract per month (Eviction Lab) and statewide counts (RIHousing).
**Fallback:** an APRA request to RIHousing (recordsrequests@rihousing.com) for the monthly court extract that feeds its evictions dashboard, property address and case dates only.

> Report requested: all residential eviction (landlord-tenant) cases filed in the Sixth Division District Court for properties in the City of Providence, January 1, 2016 to the present. Fields: case number, filing date, case type (nonpayment, other), property street address, disposition, disposition date, judgment type (possession, money, both), and judgment amount. Format: CSV or Excel. Please omit defendant names; plaintiff names are not needed. Purpose: research on rental housing conditions in Providence.

## 4. Code-enforcement history (Inspections & Standards)

**Send:** https://providenceri.nextrequest.com/ (department: Inspections & Standards)
**Why:** the public snapshots stop at 999 records (2022–2024) and 1,269 active cases. A full history shows building condition over time.

> Under the Access to Public Records Act, I request an electronic export (CSV or Excel) of all code-enforcement (housing, building and zoning) case records opened from January 1, 2016 to the present, all statuses. For each case please include: record number, property address, plat/lot, complaint type, date of first inspection, violation codes cited, notice of violation issue and release dates, condemnation and intent-to-condemn dates, status, and closed date. Owner names and complainant information are not needed. Please also release the vacant and abandoned property registry (as released for request 25-339) for 2025 and 2026, plat/lot and address only.

## 5. Affordable-housing inventory by property (RIHousing and the Executive Office of Housing)

**Send:** RIHousing, recordsrequests@rihousing.com (or the APRA form at https://www.rihousing.com, 44 Washington St, Providence, RI 02903). Send the same text to the Executive Office of Housing (https://housing.ri.gov/about-us/contact-us), which took over the low- and moderate-income housing chart in 2025.
**Why:** exemptions (i)/(j) cover deed-restricted and subsidized units. We have HUD's lists, but not the state's full inventory with affordability end dates.

> Under the Access to Public Records Act, I request the property-level Low and Moderate Income Housing (LMIH) inventory for the City of Providence used to prepare the 2025 LMIH chart under R.I.G.L. 45-53, in electronic form (CSV or Excel): property name, street address, plat/lot if recorded, total units, restricted units by income level, subsidy or program type, tenure (rental or ownership), affordability start date and end (expiration) date, and the recorded deed restriction reference. No tenant information is requested.
