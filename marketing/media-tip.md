A Providence resident built a free app to make 311 reporting a 30-second job

## Tip

A Providence resident has quietly launched **SnapPVD** (SNAPPVD_URL), a free tool that lets people file a city 311 report from their phone in about 30 seconds — pick the problem, snap a photo, confirm the address, and send.

It covers the everyday stuff residents already report: potholes, missed trash and recycling pickups, broken street lights, illegal dumping, abandoned vehicles, parking issues, and more. Reports are relayed into the city's official 311 system, and each reporter gets a link to track their request. There's a public map of recent reports, and the tool is available in Spanish.

It's a volunteer community project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the City of Providence. A person reviews each report before it's filed, and it's not meant for emergencies — residents are directed to call 911 for anything urgent.
